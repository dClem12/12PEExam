Frequently asked questions

Are marks official?

No. Teacher judgement applies.

Do hints reduce marks?

No; attempts are labelled independent or supported.

What is included for free?

Ten assessed submissions and saved progress.

What does $15 provide?

12 months of access with no automatic renewal.

Can I sit the full exam?

Yes. Exam mode includes all Section A and Section B questions.

Can I filter by topic?

Yes. Practice and Exam include topic filters such as Biomechanics and Energy Systems.

Can feedback be reviewed?

Yes. Teacher judgement overrides automated feedback.

Can I export or delete data?

Yes, through Profile.

Is this affiliated with VCAA?

No.

Independent educational prototype. Not affiliated with or endorsed by VCAA. Marks are indicative.
